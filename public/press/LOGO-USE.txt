MUGSY'S MUGS - LOGO USE

Files
- mugsys-logo-yellow.svg  yellow square, dark mug (default)
- mugsys-logo-blue.svg    blue square, yellow mug
- mugsys-logo-ink.svg     near-black square, yellow mug
- mugsys-logo-white.svg   white square, dark mug (for dark or blue backgrounds)

Colours
Blue #183fad | Mid blue #4565bc | Yellow #F1BF0A | Ice #e9ecf6 | Ink #090909 | Soft blue #abb9de

Please
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to a quarter of its width.
- Use the mark at 24px or larger.
- Use the files as supplied.

Please don't
- Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
- Add shadows, outlines or effects.
- Place it on a busy photograph without a plain backing.
